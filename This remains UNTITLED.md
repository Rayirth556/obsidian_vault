I gave trust freely.  
I let people in, not because I was weak, but because I believed in connection, loyalty, and shared growth.  
I took on their habits, their jokes, their music, their late-night talks, believing it was friendship.  
It wasn’t. It was dependency in disguise.

What hurts isn’t just the betrayal of an idea or being excluded.  
It’s realizing that the people who shaped my days were trying to mold a version of me they could use, not the one they could value.  
The jokes that went too far, the banter that actually hurt, the silence I kept, all of it was me convincing myself that maybe I was overreacting.  
But I wasn’t.

I see it now.  
I was never too sensitive.  
I was surrounded by people too shallow to understand what sensitivity really is.

Kindness was always my strength.  
It still is. But today, I lost trust in kindness.  
Because the world has a way of twisting it, of taking what’s genuine and making you feel stupid for offering it.  
I gave too much, too easily, thinking that care would be returned just because it was real.

It wasn’t.  
And that’s what hurts most, not losing an idea, but losing that innocent part of me that thought kindness was always safe.

But they didn’t destroy my kindness. They just showed me where not to give it.  
They reminded me that not everyone deserves the unfiltered version of who I am.  
From now on, my kindness will have boundaries, not walls, but gates that only open for people who never make me question my worth.

This won’t break me.  
It’s my first betrayal, not my last. But it’s the one that changes me for good.  
I’ll still trust, but I’ll be careful.  
I’ll still love, but I’ll pay attention to how it’s returned.  
And I’ll still create, but this time, I’m doing it for myself first.

What they took was an idea.  
What they lost was me.

That’s a trade they’ll regret long after I’ve moved on.  
I’m not walking away bitter. I’m walking away awake.  
They took something small, but I got something far greater in return : **clarity**.  
And that clarity will take me places no false friendship ever could.
